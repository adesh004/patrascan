Privacy policy

App: PatraScan: PDF Scanner & Vault (app.patrascan) · Developer: TechCoderHub · Effective: 18 November 2026

PatraScan is built so that your documents stay yours. We do not run a server, we do not have accounts, and we cannot see your documents.

What stays on your phone

  • Documents you scan or add, their previews, names, types, the text read from them (OCR), family-member labels, expiry dates and your saved signature are stored only in the app's private storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept in Android's secure storage.
  • Text recognition, QR reading, edge detection, filters, PDF editing and compression all run on your phone. Nothing is uploaded for them.
  • An optional app PIN is stored only as a salted one-way hash.

What we collect

Nothing. PatraScan contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting. We do not collect personal data, device identifiers, location or usage data.

When data leaves your phone

  • When you share or save a copy. You choose the app or folder; that copy is no longer protected by PatraScan.
  • Google Drive sync (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, PatraScan uses your own Google account with the drive.appdata permission only: a hidden folder that only PatraScan can see. Files are uploaded already encrypted; Google sees only file sizes, counts and times, never names or contents. The key to open them is protected by a recovery key that only you have. We never see your Google data; PatraScan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Backup file (optional). A file you create, locked with a password you choose, saved wherever you pick.
  • Smart scan uses Google Play services' on-device document scanner; Google Play services may download the scanner the first time it is used.

Permissions

  • Camera (through the system camera or scanner) — to scan documents.
  • Photos and files (system pickers) — only the items you choose.
  • Notifications — expiry reminders you set. They never show document names.
  • Internet — only for optional Drive sync.
  • Biometrics — optional fingerprint unlock, handled by Android.

Deleting your data

Delete documents in the app, or uninstall PatraScan to remove everything on the phone. To remove the Drive copy, turn off sync and delete PatraScan's hidden data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → PatraScan → Delete hidden app data).

Children

PatraScan is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
